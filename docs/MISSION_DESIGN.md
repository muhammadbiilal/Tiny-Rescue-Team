# Mission Authoring and 365-Campaign Contract

The 365-row CSV/JSON manifest specifies district, teaching phase, hazard, team size, objective and twist. It is a **production queue**. Each ID requires a finished playable encounter scene: original layout, path graph, spawn/deployment zones, hazards with schedules, civilian needs, capacity/equipment, team constraints, win/fail predicates, initial economy allowance, timing and difficulty tuning, tutorial/copy, intended strategies, replay trace and editorial approval.

## Progression cadence
Slots 1–4 introduce; 5–10 practice; 11–20 combine role synergies; 21–29 advanced variations; 30 district finale. Worlds 1–12 span missions 001–360; finale 361–365. Across each 30: 10 straightforward, 12 moderate, 6 hard, 2 mastery; do not label hard by arbitrary stat inflation. New systems appear with safe practice, then combine with prior systems. Avoid duplicate setups differing only in colors or numbers.

## Generator and validation
Authors may use procedural layout suggestions, but output is curated. Deterministic simulation/solver or bounded search verifies at least one free-player completion path for each mission and required upgrades. Replay witness must win under release rules. Static checks detect unreachable targets, absent prerequisites, broken capacities, incompatible responder equipment, ineffective player choices and near-duplicate layouts. Automated solvability does not replace human playtests. Record mission video, human feedback, difficulty metrics and signoff per ID.

## Offline contract
All 365 approved missions and assets bundled in base app or install-time asset pack guaranteed available before level selection; no online content fetch required. If size forces packs, choose a verified offline delivery strategy and test install/first launch in airplane mode. Local save uses stable IDs and schema migrations.
