Each role has base capability plus 2 branching upgrade paths, each with 3 meaningful tiers; no infinite stat inflation. Upgrades alter tactical options or efficiency within bounded tuning. Starter team must complete first 30 missions without currency purchase. New roles unlock by progression. All 365 missions must be solvable with earned resources by a free player. Avoid random gacha, pay-to-win, idle timers and energy gates.

## Progression economy
Earn service tokens through first completion and modest replay mastery; transparent fixed upgrade costs with total economy spreadsheet and simulation test. Refund/respec available without payment. Gear is earned through mission milestones, no randomized paid boxes. Difficulty balanced against minimum earned progression, not maximum grind. Document difficulty envelope and automated free-player completion path.

## Character identity
Give each role an original name, personality and short safe dialogue; avoid real agency logos or official affiliation. Include character sheet with silhouette, palette, front/side/back or 8-direction needs, idle/run/action/react/victory states, hitbox and animation timing. Diverse characters, no graphic injury.
